About & Credits

Tattoo of the World is an open, illustrated atlas of tattoo traditions — a reading room for the oldest art form people carry on themselves.

The project

Humans have tattooed each other for more than five thousand years: the oldest preserved tattoos belong to Ötzi the Iceman (c. 3250 BC) and to the Gebelein mummies of predynastic Egypt. This site collects ten traditions — their tools, meanings and keepers — as short illustrated essays. It is a static site, hand-built and hosted on GitHub Pages.

A note on respect

Several traditions here — tā moko above all — are living cultural property, not a style menu. Where a tradition distinguishes between its own marks and art made for outsiders, that distinction is noted in the essay. Read first, ink later.
